### What's New in Cinemora v2.3.2 🎬

- 📚 **Library Screen**: Your personal collection (Watchlist, Favorites, and Watch History) is now officially your **Library**, featuring dedicated media category filters and search history.
- 🔍 **Search Tab**: Dedicated Search screen accessible right from the bottom navigation bar for quick movie and TV discovery.
- 🖤 **AMOLED Pitch Black**: Restored classic 3-way Theme switcher (**System**, **Dark**, **Light**) with a dedicated toggle switch for pure `#000000` pitch black surfaces on OLED displays.
- 🔲 **Edge-to-Edge Fullscreen**: True immersive display layout with full support for Android gesture navigation and camera cutouts with zero letterboxing.
- 🎬 **Cinema App Icon**: High-definition 3D cinema clapperboard and glowing film reel launcher icon.
- 🔄 **Enhanced In-App Updater**: What's New release notes are now displayed directly inside the app when checking for updates.
- 🔐 **Permanent Keystore**: Standardized release signing certificate ensuring seamless update installations without package signature conflicts.
