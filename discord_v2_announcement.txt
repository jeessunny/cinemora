🎉 **Cinemora V2 is Officially Live!** 🎉

The ultimate Customization Engine update has arrived! We've been working hard today to give you complete control over how the app looks and feels. 

🚀 **NEW FEATURES**

**1. True Color Customization** 🎨
Tired of the default Netflix Red? You can now change the app's primary accent color to absolutely anything you want using a beautiful color picker. This updates buttons, toggles, text, and icons globally in real-time!

**2. Floating Navigation Bar** ✨
You can now toggle between a traditional fixed bottom navigation bar and a sleek, premium, iOS-style floating pill! It morphs between the two states with a buttery-smooth spring animation.

**3. Navbar Tab Customization** 🛠️
Want a cleaner interface? You can now dynamically hide or show the **Search** and **Settings** tabs from your navbar. The remaining icons smoothly slide and adjust to stay perfectly centered!

**4. Home Screen Layout Control** 🏠
You now have the power to reorganize your entire home screen! Use the brand-new drag-and-drop system in Settings to reorder sections like "Trending Now", "New Releases", and "Because You Like Action".

**5. Advanced Animation Kill-Switch** ⚡
Not a fan of animations? We added a master "All Animations" toggle. Turning this off globally disables every layout shift, page transition, and hover effect for lightning-fast navigation. 

**6. Water Ripple Effects** 💧
We built a custom, premium water ripple effect for the navigation bar icons that perfectly tracks where your finger taps! (And yes, you can toggle this off individually if you prefer!).

**7. Massive Bug Fixes & Polish** 🐛🔨
- Fixed the "ash color" glitch on Segmented Buttons
- Prevented crash loops when mashing the back button on the profile screen
- Improved history tracking and history stack management
- Removed ugly hover glows for a cleaner Material 3 look
